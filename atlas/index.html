A WORLD WORTH KNOWING

A world geography quiz for curious minds.

Atlas is a free geography game for learning countries, capital cities and flags on an interactive globe. Choose Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, South America, Oceania or the whole world. Take a random 5, 10 or 25-place quiz, or explore the country study guide at your own pace.

Practice locating countries, identify capitals with choices from the same country, and explore flags, country outlines, languages, religions and government types. Play in standard or hard mode on your phone, tablet or computer. Atlas includes 195 countries, 12 territories and 2 disputed places; progress and explorer titles are saved in your browser.

Created out of love for world geography by Ron Domingue · rondomingue.com ↗195 countries + 12 territories + 2 disputed places · One extraordinary planet

YOUR FIELD GUIDE

Find your place
in the world.

Choose a continent for a shorter expedition, or take on all 195 countries in the world. Switch to Countries & territories to include 12 additional places, including French Guiana. Every place in your selection appears once, in a shuffled order.

  1. Read the country’s name and flag. Turn on Flags only for an extra challenge.
  2. Drag to rotate the globe. Zoom in, then click a country’s shape. Small countries have a larger dot to make them easier to select.
  3. Check your answer. Green reveals the correct country; coral marks a missed guess. Keep going, or practise your misses at the end.

Keyboard: focus the globe, use arrow keys to pan and + / − to zoom. Press Enter to select the country under the centre crosshair.

Correct answers give a gentle country glow; full profiles expand inline only when you choose them. Hard mode hides selected-country names and previously found country colors. Combine it with Flags only for an extra challenge. Sound on/off is saved on this device.

Your title advances from Scout to Pioneer, Explorer, Voyager and beyond as you identify unique places correctly. Repeat finds count once. Your expedition saves automatically on this device. Completed continents and personal bests stay with you.

THE FINE PRINT

A world of detail.

This collection includes the 193 United Nations member states, plus Palestine and Vatican City, for 195 countries. The optional expanded collection adds French Guiana, Greenland, Puerto Rico, Bermuda, Aruba, Curaçao, Cayman Islands, Falkland Islands, French Polynesia, New Caledonia, Gibraltar and Faroe Islands. These are labeled as territories rather than sovereign countries.

Countries are assigned to one continent for quiz purposes: Russia to Europe; Türkiye, Cyprus, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Kazakhstan to Asia. Central America and the Caribbean belong to the North America expedition.

Country profiles include languages, religions and government classifications from the World Factbook archive, with supplementary sources linked in each full profile. This is an archival reference, not a live government directory; estimate years are retained where supplied.

Country metadata: mledoze/countries. Boundaries: Natural Earth / geo-countries. Flag images: Flagcdn. Boundaries are generalised geographic representations.
