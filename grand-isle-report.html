Highway 1 & Road Conditions
Live traffic in Google Maps ↗ 511 Louisiana road conditions ↗
Know before you go: LA Highway 1 is the only road on and off Grand Isle. The Leeville and Fourchon stretches are prone to overwash at high tide, during onshore wind events, and in tropical weather — check 511la.org before a trip if tides are running high or a storm is nearby.
