A rewritten ballot

For 2026 Louisiana replaced its 50-year-old jungle primary with closed party primaries. In the first test, two-term Sen. Bill Cassidy was knocked out — the Secretary of State projected turnout as low as 20–25%. Ballotpedia, Louisiana Illuminator.

What one vote can doMargins that changed the outcome

Since 1976, more than 85 U.S. Senate elections were decided by less than three percent of the vote. A handful of these came down to a margin you could fit in a single precinct.
