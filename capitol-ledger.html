Method note
This prototype uses a small curated demonstration dataset to show the product model and interface. It does not allege misconduct. Official disclosure records are treated as primary inputs; return, gain, excess-return, and significance fields are calculated estimates that require refreshed market data before publication.

[ Separate valuation lanes ]
Crypto licensing income, token-sale income, public-company equity, founder allocations, and corporate treasury holdings are not combined into one portfolio return. Each lane carries its own source, ownership path, and confidence rating.

Purchase estimate formula: shares = midpoint / adjusted close, then gain = shares x current price - midpoint.

Founder or promotional allocation: value is shown as estimated market value only. Cost basis is unavailable unless source documentation provides it.

Corporate treasury: company-held crypto is displayed as corporate exposure; indirect exposure requires a reliable ownership percentage.

[ Source and adapter plan ]
Primary records should be official disclosure filings: House and Senate PTRs, annual disclosures, Office of Government Ethics reports, SEC EDGAR filings, and issuer documents. Third-party trading APIs can normalize records for prototyping but should retain original filing links.
Market data should flow through provider adapters for adjusted historical close, current price, ticker history, splits, dividends, company metadata, and benchmark series.
Recent reporting used for the executive-branch/crypto scope should be rechecked against primary documents before publication. See: U.S. Office of Government Ethics, SEC EDGAR, and official congressional disclosure portals.
